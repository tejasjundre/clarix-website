The problem

Too many schools still run critical operations on paper

Fee follow-up sits in registers, attendance stays in paper sheets, and parent communication depends on scattered calls or notice boards. That creates avoidable delays, missed updates, and extra work for school teams.

What schools face

Manual work slows the day and limits visibility

Clarix starts with the admin workflows that create the most pressure for principals, owners, and office teams.

  • Fee collection follow-up becomes hard to track consistently
  • Attendance records stay disconnected from reporting and review
  • Parent communication gets delayed during busy school days
  • Admin teams repeat the same work across registers, sheets, and calls
Why now

Schools need affordable digitization, not bulky enterprise ERP

Clarix is being designed for Indian private schools that want better control, cleaner operations, and faster communication without enterprise-level complexity.

For principals Daily visibility without chasing updates manually
For admin teams Less paperwork and fewer repeated entries
For parents Faster reminders and more reliable communication
For growth Start with core operations and expand in phases
